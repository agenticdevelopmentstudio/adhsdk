This ingredient sets the app's text contrast floors, so accessibility applies to its output even though it draws nothing. The floors (minimum contrast 3.0 for `secondaryText` and `userName`, 2.0 for `tertiaryText` and `timestampText`, 1.6 for `placeholderText`) are recorded under Behavioral Requirements. They intentionally fall below WCAG AA's 4.5 for normal text, because those roles are de-emphasised text; only `primaryText`, `onAccentText` and `selectionText` are chosen for strong contrast. The Compliance table records the matching contrast-ratio check as partial.
